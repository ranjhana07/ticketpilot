# Export your workspace data

Owners can download a full copy of a workspace.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings > General > Export data**.
2. Choose JSON, or CSV plus attachments.
3. Select **Start export**.

You get an email when the archive is ready, usually within an hour. The link works for seven days. Large workspaces with many attachments can take up to a day.

## If the export times out

Use the project level export from the project menu and combine the files, or ask support to run the export for you. We will check that the request comes from an owner.

## Personal data requests

You can ask for the personal data we hold about you at any time, and ask for it to be deleted. Send the request from your account email to privacy@loomwork.dev. We answer within 30 days.
