# Update your payment method

Open **Settings > Billing > Payment method** and select **Replace card**. Cards from Visa, Mastercard and American Express are supported. We also accept SEPA direct debit for annual plans in the EU.

## Notes

- The new card is used for the next renewal. It is not charged immediately.
- If a payment is currently failing, replacing the card triggers a new attempt within the hour. See [Failed payments](failed-payments.md).
- Support never asks for a full card number by email or chat. If you send one by mistake we delete the message and recommend you ask your bank to replace the card.
