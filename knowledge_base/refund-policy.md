# Refund policy

We want you to be able to try Loomwork without risk.

## What is refundable

- Subscription charges within 30 days of the charge date, for monthly and annual plans.
- Add-on orders (extra seats, storage packs) that were not provisioned, or that were bought by mistake within 30 days.
- Duplicate charges, at any time.

## What is not refundable

- Charges older than 30 days.
- The unused part of a billing period after you cancel. Cancelling stops the next renewal and the plan stays active until the period ends.
- Orders that have already been refunded in full.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

## Approval

Refunds up to 50 USD are processed after we verify the account owner. Larger refunds are reviewed by a support lead before they are issued. Amounts above 500 USD are handled by the billing team and can take up to three business days.
