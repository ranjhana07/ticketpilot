# Cancel your subscription

Owners can cancel under **Settings > Billing > Plan > Cancel subscription**.

## What cancelling does

- Stops the next renewal.
- Keeps the paid plan active until the end of the current period.
- Moves the workspace to the Free plan afterwards. Data is kept, but limits of the Free plan apply.

Cancelling does not refund the current period. If you were charged in the last 30 days and want your money back, see the [refund policy](refund-policy.md).

## Cancelling through support

Support can cancel for you after verifying the account owner. Cancellations requested by support are reviewed by a team member before they are applied, because they cannot be undone from our side.

## Delete the workspace instead

If you want all data removed as well, delete the workspace under **Settings > General > Delete workspace**. Deletion is permanent after 30 days.
