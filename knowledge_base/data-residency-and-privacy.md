# Data storage, residency and privacy

## Where data is stored

New workspaces are stored in the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Virginia). You choose the region when you create the workspace and it cannot be changed afterwards. Backups stay in the same region.

## Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or newer and at rest with AES-256. Enterprise customers can bring their own keys.

## Retention and deletion

- Deleted tasks and projects stay in the trash for 30 days.
- A deleted workspace is removed permanently after 30 days.
- Backups are purged within 90 days.

## Compliance

Loomwork has a data processing agreement for customers in the EU and UK. Request it from privacy@loomwork.dev. The list of sub-processors is available on request.
