# Unlock a locked account

Loomwork locks an account to protect it when we see signs of misuse.

## Why an account gets locked

- Five failed sign-in attempts within ten minutes.
- Five rejected two-factor codes in a row.
- Sign-ins from several countries within a short time.
- A payment dispute that is still open with your card issuer.

## Automatic unlock

Locks caused by failed sign-ins lift on their own after 30 minutes. Wait, then sign in again with the correct password. Using **Forgot password** resets the counter immediately.

## When the lock stays

If you see the message "Your account has been suspended", the lock was applied by our security team and will not lift on its own. Contact support from the email address on the account. We will verify your identity before we change anything and will tell you what triggered the lock.

Owners of a workspace can also see which members are locked under **Settings > Members**.
