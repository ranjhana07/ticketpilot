# Upgrade your plan

Owners can upgrade at any time under **Settings > Billing > Plan**.

## What happens to billing

- The new plan starts immediately.
- We credit the unused part of your current period and charge the new plan pro rata for the rest of it.
- Your billing date does not change.
- Features of the new plan unlock straight away.

## Example

You are on Team monthly and move to Business with 10 seats halfway through the month. You pay the price difference for the remaining half month, then 180 USD at the next renewal.

## Before you upgrade

Check whether your team needs SSO or the audit log, which are available from Business upwards. Move to annual billing in the same step to save two months of the price. See [Annual billing](annual-billing.md).
