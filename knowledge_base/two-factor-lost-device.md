# Lost your 2FA device

If you can no longer open your authenticator app you can still get back in.

## Use a recovery code

On the two-factor prompt choose **Use a recovery code** and enter one of the ten codes you saved when you enabled 2FA. After signing in, go to **Profile > Security** and set up your new device.

## No recovery codes

Contact support from the email address on the account. Because 2FA protects against takeover, we do not remove it on a simple request. We will ask you to confirm details that only the account owner knows, and for workspace members the workspace owner may be asked to confirm.

Removal takes up to one business day. Once it is removed you will be asked to set up 2FA again at your next sign-in.

## Prevent it next time

Store recovery codes in a password manager and keep a second device registered if your authenticator app supports it.
