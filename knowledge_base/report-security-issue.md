# Report suspicious activity or a security issue

## Suspicious sign-ins or changes in your workspace

1. Change your password and sign out all sessions under **Profile > Security > Sessions**.
2. Turn on two-factor authentication.
3. Review **Settings > Audit log** (Business plans) for changes you did not make.
4. Revoke API tokens you do not recognise. See [API tokens](api-tokens.md).
5. Contact support and tell us the times and countries you saw.

Security reports are treated as high priority and answered by a person within one hour on business days.

## Phishing

Loomwork emails come from loomwork.dev. We never ask for your password or a card number. Forward suspicious messages to security@loomwork.dev with the full headers.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Send details to security@loomwork.dev. Please do not open a public issue or test against other customers' data. We acknowledge reports within two business days and credit researchers who follow coordinated disclosure.
