# VAT and tax IDs

Loomwork charges VAT or sales tax where the law requires it.

## Add a VAT number

Go to **Settings > Billing > Company details** and enter the VAT or tax ID. EU businesses with a valid VAT ID are not charged VAT on new invoices under the reverse charge rule. The ID is checked against the VIES service and can take a few minutes to validate.

## Past invoices

A tax ID added now does not change invoices that were already issued. Ask support to reissue an invoice with the ID, and we will send a corrected copy. See [Find and download invoices](invoices-and-receipts.md).

## US sales tax

Sales tax is added at checkout based on the billing address for states where we are registered. Tax-exempt organisations can send their certificate to support.
