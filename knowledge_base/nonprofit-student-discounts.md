# Discounts for non-profits and students

## Non-profits and open source

Registered non-profit organisations and open source maintainers get 50 percent off the Team plan. Send proof of registration, or a link to the project repository, to support. Discounts are applied to the next invoice and need to be renewed every year.

## Students and educators

Students and teachers can use the Team plan for free for personal projects. Apply with an email address from your school. The workspace is limited to five members and cannot be used for commercial work.

## What is not covered

The discounts do not apply to Business or Enterprise plans or to add-on packs. They cannot be combined with other offers.
