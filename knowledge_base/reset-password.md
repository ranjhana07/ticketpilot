# Reset your password

If you cannot sign in to Loomwork, you can set a new password yourself in under two minutes.

## Steps

1. Open the sign-in page and choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address you registered with.
3. Open the message titled "Reset your Loomwork password" and click the link.
4. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters.

## If the email does not arrive

- Check spam and any quarantine your company mail filter keeps.
- Make sure you used the address on the account. Workspace aliases do not receive reset links.
- Links expire 60 minutes after they are sent, and only the most recent link works. Request a new one if yours has expired.

Accounts with two-factor authentication still need the authenticator code after the reset. If you lost the device, see [Lost your 2FA device](two-factor-lost-device.md).

Support can send a fresh reset link once we have verified that you own the account. We never set a password on your behalf and we never ask you to tell us your password.
