# Find and download invoices

Every charge produces an invoice and a receipt.

## Download from the workspace

1. Open **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.
2. Choose the invoice and select **Download PDF**.

Only owners and billing contacts can see this page.

## Resend an invoice

Select **Email me this invoice** to receive a copy at the billing contact address. If an invoice never arrived, check the billing contact address and spam first.

## Correct company details

To change the company name, address or VAT number on an invoice, update **Settings > Billing > Company details** and then ask support to reissue the invoice. Invoices cannot be edited after the fact, so we issue a corrected copy and keep the original on file.

Invoice numbers match the order numbers shown in your order history.
