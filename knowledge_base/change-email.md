# Change your account email

You can move your account to a new email address without losing any data.

## Steps

1. Open **Profile > Account**.
2. Select **Change email** and enter the new address.
3. Confirm using the link we send to the new address within 24 hours.
4. We also notify the old address so that unexpected changes are visible.

## Things to check

- The new address must not belong to another Loomwork account.
- Invoices are sent to the billing contact, which is set separately under **Settings > Billing**.
- If you cannot read mail at the old address and lost access to the account, contact support. We will verify ownership before changing the address.

Single sign-on users change their email in their identity provider, and Loomwork picks it up at the next sign-in.
