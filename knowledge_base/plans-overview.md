# Plans and pricing

| | Free | Team | Business | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price per seat per month | 0 USD | 9 USD | 18 USD | Custom |
| Price per seat per year | 0 USD | 90 USD | 180 USD | Custom |
| Members | Up to 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Storage | 2 GB | 50 GB per workspace | 500 GB per workspace | Custom |
| Gantt and timeline views | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Single sign-on | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Audit log | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Support | Help center | Email, 1 business day | Email and chat, 4 hours | Dedicated contact |

Guests are free on every plan.

## Enterprise

Enterprise plans include custom contracts, data residency options and a named support contact. Contact sales; plan changes to Enterprise are handled by the sales team and cannot be made from the billing page.

See [Upgrade your plan](upgrade-plan.md) and [Downgrade your plan](downgrade-plan.md) to move between plans.
