# Add-on orders and their status

Add-ons are purchases on top of your plan: extra seats, storage packs and the advanced reporting pack. Each one gets an order number such as ORD-48213.

## Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| pending | Payment is being confirmed. Usually under 15 minutes |
| paid | Payment confirmed, activation is running |
| provisioned | The add-on is live in your workspace |
| refunded | Fully refunded |
| failed | Payment did not go through |

## When an order seems stuck

If an order stays on **paid** for more than one hour, the add-on was probably not activated. Support can check the order, re-run the activation, or refund it if you prefer. Quote the order number in your message.

Seat packs show up under **Settings > Members** as soon as they are provisioned. Storage packs raise the limit shown under **Settings > Usage**.
