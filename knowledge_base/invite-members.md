# Invite and remove team members

Workspace owners and admins manage membership under **Settings > Members**.

## Invite people

1. Select **Invite members**.
2. Enter one or more email addresses, or paste a list.
3. Pick a role for each person.

Invitations expire after 14 days. You can resend or revoke a pending invitation from the same page.

## Seats and billing

Each member counts as a billable seat on Team and Business plans. Guests do not use a seat. Adding a member mid-cycle is charged pro rata for the remaining days. Removing a member lowers the seat count at the next renewal and does not issue a credit.

## Remove a member

Choose **Remove** next to their name. Their tasks stay in the workspace and become unassigned. If you want to keep a record of what they worked on, deactivate instead of removing.
