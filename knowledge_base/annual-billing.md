# Switch to annual billing

Annual billing costs ten times the monthly price, which is two months free.

## Switch

1. Open **Settings > Billing > Plan**.
2. Select **Billing cycle** and choose **Annual**.
3. Confirm the charge.

The switch takes effect immediately. We credit the unused part of your current monthly period against the annual price, so you are not charged twice for the same days.

## Seats during the year

Seats added mid-year are charged pro rata until the annual renewal date. Removing seats does not reduce the annual charge until renewal.

## Back to monthly

You can change back to monthly at renewal time. We do not refund the unused part of an annual period, other than within the 30 day window described in the [refund policy](refund-policy.md).
