# Notification settings

Notifications can arrive in the app, by email and in the mobile app.

## Choose what you receive

Open **Profile > Notifications**. For each event type, such as comments, mentions, assignments and due dates, turn on the channels you want.

## Duplicate notifications

Getting the same message twice usually means both the email and the mobile push are on for the same event, or you belong to the project through two teams. Turn off one channel, or leave the extra team. A comment that mentions you in a thread you already follow generates one notification, not two.

## Digest emails

Switch **Email digest** on to receive a single summary at 9:00 in your time zone, instead of one email per event.

## Mute a project

Open the project menu and choose **Mute**. Mentions still reach you.
