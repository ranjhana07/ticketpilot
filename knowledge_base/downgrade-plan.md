# Downgrade your plan

You can move to a cheaper plan from **Settings > Billing > Plan**.

## When it takes effect

A downgrade starts at the end of the current billing period, so you keep the features you paid for until then. No refund is issued for the remaining time.

## What to check first

- **Free** allows up to five members. Remove or deactivate the rest before the change or the downgrade will not go through.
- Storage above the new plan limit becomes read-only until you delete files or upgrade again.
- Business features such as SSO, custom roles and the audit log are switched off. Members who sign in with SSO need a password set before the switch.

## Change your mind

You can cancel a scheduled downgrade any time before it takes effect, from the same page.
