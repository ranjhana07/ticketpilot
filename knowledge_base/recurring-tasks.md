# Recurring tasks

A recurring task creates a new copy when the previous one is completed or when the next interval starts.

## Set one up

1. Open the task and select **Repeat**.
2. Choose daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.
3. Pick whether the next task is created on a schedule or after completion.

## Custom intervals

Custom intervals such as every two weeks on Tuesday are available. Intervals shorter than a day and rules like the last working day of the month are not supported yet. You can follow the request on the public roadmap and vote for it.

## Tips

- Subtasks and checklists are copied to each new instance.
- Assignees are kept. Due dates move forward by the length of the interval.
- To stop a series, open the task and choose **Stop repeating**.
