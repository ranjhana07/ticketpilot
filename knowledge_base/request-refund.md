# How to request a refund

Contact support from the email address that owns the account and include:

- The order number, which starts with ORD- and is shown on the invoice.
- The amount you want refunded.
- A short reason, for example duplicate charge or add-on bought by mistake.

## What happens next

1. We verify that the request comes from the account owner or a billing contact.
2. We check the order against the [refund policy](refund-policy.md).
3. Small refunds are issued straight away. Larger ones wait for a support lead.
4. You get a confirmation email with the refund reference.

## Partial refunds

You can ask for part of an order to be refunded, for example when you added five seats and only need two. The refund cannot be larger than the order total minus anything already refunded.

If the order is older than 30 days we cannot refund it, but we can pass your case to the billing team for review.
